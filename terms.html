Terms & Conditions

Working with Zesix Studio

Payment

50% advance before a project starts. For applicable single-deliverable and print projects, the remaining 50% is due before final files. Paid amounts are non-refundable.

Revisions

Up to 3 sets of revisions per deliverable. Additional rounds can be quoted separately.

Delivery

We work to committed deadlines, with roughly 1–2 days' grace for genuine emergencies.

Cancellation

For ongoing contracts, 15 days' written notice is required; otherwise a 15-day service charge applies.

Ownership

Final ownership transfers on full payment. Until then the work remains Zesix Studio's intellectual property.

Portfolio rights

Unless agreed otherwise in writing, Zesix may showcase completed work on its website, portfolio and social media.

Confidentiality

Both parties maintain confidentiality around relevant business and project information.

Printing & production

Zesix undertakes printing and production in-house where a project needs it, and is accountable for the physical output.
